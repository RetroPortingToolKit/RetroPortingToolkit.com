These pages focus on toolchains that have a clear public direction and enough working foundation to be useful to readers today. They are not a promise that every game is ready to port.

## The standard

- [PlayStation](/docs/platforms/playstation). The most complete end-to-end path in this collection; the current gold standard for maturity.
- [SNES](/docs/platforms/snes). Another strong contender, with a particularly interesting CPU model.

## Earlier on the same road

- [NES](/docs/platforms/nes). A compact example of code discovery and mapper handling.
- [Game Boy Advance](/docs/platforms/game-boy-advance). ARM and Thumb sharing one address space.
- [Sega Genesis](/docs/platforms/sega-genesis). A two-processor system with a separate sound CPU.
- [Nintendo DS](/docs/platforms/nintendo-ds). An experimental dual-CPU path.
- Master System and Game Gear remain repository experiments rather than a supported path.

## Research projects

CD-i, GameCube and Xbox experiments remain useful reference material, but they are research projects rather than a supported route to a playable port. Their pages are retained for context, not endorsement.

Other experiments may still exist in their repositories, but they are not presented here as supported or endorsed platform paths. The [status vocabulary](/docs/reference/status-vocabulary) explains the careful language used on each page.
