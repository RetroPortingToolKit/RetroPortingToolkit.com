OpenPete is a community-built native PC port of Spyro the Dragon (NTSC-U), released at v0.1.3 for Windows with Linux and macOS planned. It is a hybrid: altro50's unfinished Spyro decompilation provides the hand-written code, [PSXRecomp](/hardware/playstation) covers the functions that have not been decompiled, and SoapyMan's Psycross supplies the platform layer. openpete.com explicitly credits psxrecomp in its "Built on" section.

## What works today

The game runs as a native PC application. Coverage in Time Extension names Matthew Stanley's PSXRecomp as the AI-assisted tool behind the recompiled portion, and ixbt.games reports play at 240 FPS.

## Enhancements

Presentation-only, per the project: widescreen, high framerate, HD textures, extended draw distances, and music and texture swaps. Gameplay is untouched.

No game data is distributed; the port builds from your own legally dumped disc image.
