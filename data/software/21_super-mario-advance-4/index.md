Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3 is the GBA release of Super Mario Bros. 3, recompiled as one of the [GBARecomp](/hardware/game-boy-advance) titles listed in the framework README. Like the rest of the lineup it is an experimental preview, early in its life and shared as a byproduct of developing the framework itself.

No game data is distributed; the project builds from your own legally dumped ROM and GBA BIOS.
