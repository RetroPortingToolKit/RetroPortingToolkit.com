xenogears-recomp is an independent community project by OpokXeno, described as a "Static recompilation of Xenogears for PC". It builds on [PSXRecomp](/hardware/playstation) as its decoder rather than reimplementing the recompilation layer, and is one of the clearest signs of the toolkit being picked up outside the core team.

## What works today

The project is in active alpha, with its most recent release at v0.5.0 (2026-08-17).

No game data is distributed; the project builds from your own legally dumped disc image.
