Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2 is the GBA release of Super Mario World, recompiled as one of the [GBARecomp](/hardware/game-boy-advance) titles listed in the framework README. Like the rest of the lineup it is an experimental preview. It makes an interesting pair with the [SNES Super Mario World recomp](/software/super-mario-world), the same game recompiled from two different architectures.

No game data is distributed; the project builds from your own legally dumped ROM and GBA BIOS.
