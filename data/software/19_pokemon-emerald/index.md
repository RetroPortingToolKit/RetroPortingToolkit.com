Pokemon Emerald completes Gen 3 coverage in the [GBARecomp](/hardware/game-boy-advance) lineup, joining [Ruby & Sapphire](/software/pokemon-ruby-sapphire) and [FireRed & LeafGreen](/software/pokemon-firered-leafgreen). The repository is public; like the rest of the framework's games it is an experimental preview, and this one is early enough that there are no release details to report yet.

No game data is distributed; the project builds from your own legally dumped ROM and GBA BIOS.
