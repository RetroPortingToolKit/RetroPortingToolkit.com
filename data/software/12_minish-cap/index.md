The Minish Cap was one of the first [GBARecomp](/hardware/game-boy-advance) titles, among the five games booting to playable states when the framework was announced in June 2026. Like the rest of the GBA lineup, it is an experimental preview.

## What works today

The game boots to a playable state through the recompiled runtime.

## Enhancements

Adaptive widescreen has been demonstrated up to 21:9 on this title, giving a genuinely wider logical view rather than a stretch. Minish Cap is one of the framework's showcase games for the feature, appearing in both the July and August 2026 ecosystem write-ups.

No game data is distributed; the project builds from your own legally dumped ROM and GBA BIOS.
