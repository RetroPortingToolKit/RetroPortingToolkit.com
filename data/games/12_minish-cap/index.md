When [GBARecomp](/hardware/game-boy-advance) was announced in June 2026, five games were already booting to playable states. The Minish Cap was one of them.

## Can I play it?

Boots to a playable state, as an experimental preview like the rest of the GBA lineup. You build from source with your own dumps.

## Enhancements

Adaptive widescreen has been demonstrated up to 21:9 on this title, giving a genuinely wider logical view rather than a stretch. Minish Cap is one of the framework's showcase games for the feature, appearing in both the July and August 2026 ecosystem write-ups.

## Requirements

Your own legally dumped ROM and GBA BIOS.
