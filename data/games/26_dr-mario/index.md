Dr. Mario runs as a native PC game through [NESRecomp](/hardware/nes).

It remains one of the simplest examples: a puzzle game you download, point at your own dump, and play.

## Playable status

Yes, in single player. Windows and experimental Linux builds are on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/mstan/DrMarioNesRecomp/releases). It is built from a dump you provide, the USA version, verified by checksum.

![One-player mode at level 00, medium speed, four viruses left in the bottle.](/data/blog/20_nesrecomp-10-titles/DrMarioRecomp_8cliR8zN4q.png)

One-player mode is tested through virus clearing. The options menu works for level, speed, and music selection. Two-player mode and ending sequences still need more verification.
