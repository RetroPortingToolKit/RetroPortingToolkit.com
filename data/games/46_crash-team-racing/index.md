Crash Team Racing has a community [PSXRecomp](/hardware/playstation) build by TechnicallyComputers.

It is not a recommended download right now. The developer has held it back from the catalog until a post-race bug is fixed.

## Playable status

Partial. The known issue is specific: after a race, Change Level fails to load the next screen. There may be other issues that still need testing.

If you want to try it anyway, builds were published for Windows, macOS, and Linux when the project was public. It builds from a disc dump you provide. OpenBIOS is used unless you supply your own retail BIOS.
