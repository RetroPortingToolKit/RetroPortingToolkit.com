Runs the Japanese GameCube fighting game Digimon Battle Chronicle \(デジモンバトルクロニクル, G2DJB2\) natively on Windows, Linux and Android. The game was released in North America and Europe as Digimon Rumble Arena 2; this project targets the Japanese disc, revision 0. Its PowerPC machine code is statically recompiled to C and built, together with this project's GameCube hardware layer, into x86-64 and ARM64 programs. No emulator, no interpreter, no BIOS. You supply your own disc image. Controllers, keyboa
