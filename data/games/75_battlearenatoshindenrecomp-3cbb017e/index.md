Battle Arena Toshinden is a Playstation 1 3D fighter that's been recompiled and enhanced with widescreen support, netplay, and selectors for two previously hidden characters: Gaia and Sho

![Gaia versus Sho in 21:9 adaptive ultrawide, with gauges extended to the edges](./submission-2.png)

## Project

Static recompilation of Battle Arena Toshinden \(USA\), PlayStation serial SCUS-94200, to native code using [PSXRecomp](/hardware/playstation) and recomp-ui.

The original title screen, presented at its native 4:3 aspect ratio.

0.1.2 is believed to be stable despite being an early release. Bug reports are welcome; see validation notes for the tested build and scope.

The game boots through its introduction and character selection into combat. The launcher includes disc setup, settings, and a Mods page.
