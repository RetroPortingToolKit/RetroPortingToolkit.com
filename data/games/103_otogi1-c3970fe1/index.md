Static recompilation of the original Xbox release. Boots the retail XBE on Linux, runs the title's own main loop, streams the menu BGM and opens a framebuffer window. Research preview: the UI draws, the 3D scene and controller input are not wired yet. You supply your own disc; no game data is included.
