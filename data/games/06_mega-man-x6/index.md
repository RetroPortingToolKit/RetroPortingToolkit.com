Mega Man X6 is one of the most complete titles in the [PSXRecomp](/hardware/playstation) ecosystem.

The big addition is the mod layer. It adapts the well-known Mega Man X6 Tweaks project by acediez, with permission, and lets you choose individual changes instead of taking one large patch.

![Mega Man X6 running from a native build](/previews/mega-man-x6.mp4)

## Playable status

Yes, released and playable. A Windows package is on the GitHub releases page.

The game is built from a dump you provide. This one expects the USA v1.1 revision of Mega Man X6. A bundled open-source BIOS boots it out of the box, and the launcher checks the disc before starting.

![Video Game Esoterica on the X6 release and its mod catalog](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IXMHXC2BLSc)

## What the recomp adds

The Tweaks adaptation is the main feature. It has more than 200 individual options, including timing changes, stage changes, and character adjustments. You tune the game piece by piece.

![The tweaks launcher](./mmx6-tweaks-launcher.png)

An English retranslation by DuoDynamo is included with his direct redistribution approval. The repository also tracks attribution and permission for the included mod work.

![Community mods in action](/covers/mmx6-mods.jpg)

The framework's own additions sit alongside the mod layer. Experimental 16:9 widescreen draws more of the scene on both sides instead of stretching the 4:3 picture. FMV skips are included too.

![Native 16:9 presentation](./mmx6-16x9.png)
