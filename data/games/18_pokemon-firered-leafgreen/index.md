Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen share one core [GBARecomp](/hardware/game-boy-advance) repository, and each builds as its own separate native target.

## Can I play it?

Both games build and run as experimental previews at v0.0.3 (2026-07-17), one native executable per game version.

## Requirements

Your own legally dumped ROMs and GBA BIOS.
