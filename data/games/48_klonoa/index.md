Klonoa: Door to Phantomile has a community [PSXRecomp](/hardware/playstation) build by TechnicallyComputers.

It is an alpha. The main goal is still getting the game covered and stable.

## Playable status

In alpha. Builds are published for Windows, macOS, and Linux. The project makes no claim about a complete playthrough.

It builds from a disc dump you provide. OpenBIOS boots it unless you point it at your own retail BIOS.
