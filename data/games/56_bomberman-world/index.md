Bomberman World has a community [PSXRecomp](/hardware/playstation) build by TechnicallyComputers.

It is the adventure-style Bomberman game in this group, with stages, crystals, bosses, and the original five-player battle mode.

## Playable status

Tech demo. Builds are published for Windows, Linux, and macOS, but the project does not make a clear playthrough claim.

The release builds the game locally from a disc dump you provide. OpenBIOS is used unless you point it at your own retail BIOS.

The catalog declares five-player netplay through [recomp-net](/blog/recomp-net), matching the original multitap player count. Treat that as early until the project gives clearer validation notes.
