Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2 is the GBA release of Super Mario World, one of the [GBARecomp](/hardware/game-boy-advance) titles listed in the framework README. It pairs neatly with the [SNES Super Mario World recomp](/games/super-mario-world): the same game, recompiled from two different architectures.

## Can I play it?

Source is public as an experimental preview, like the rest of the lineup; no release build is cited.

## Requirements

Your own legally dumped ROM and GBA BIOS.
