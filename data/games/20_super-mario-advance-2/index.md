Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2 is the GBA release of Super Mario World, recompiled as one of the [GBARecomp](/hardware/game-boy-advance) titles listed in the framework README, and like the rest of the lineup an experimental preview. It pairs neatly with the [SNES Super Mario World recomp](/games/super-mario-world): the same game, recompiled from two different architectures.

No game data is distributed; the project builds from your own legally dumped ROM and GBA BIOS.
