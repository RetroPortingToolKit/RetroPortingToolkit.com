Lagoon returns on Windows with widescreen exploration, an in-game map and quality-of-life improvements. And apparently someone took a wrong turn on the way to another RPG… Bring your own clean USA ROM. No ROM is included.

## Project

A Windows port of Lagoon \(USA\) built with [SNESRecomp](/hardware/super-nintendo). Native gameplay runs alongside an interpreter safety net, with widescreen, a custom scene renderer and optional quality-of-life improvements. Bring your own clean USA ROM. No ROM or extracted Lagoon graphics are bundled.

The current Windows release is 0.2.5 / v0.2.5. It fixes map text losing pixel rows during overlay presentation, keeping small lettering readable at standard and ultrawide aspects. It also includes the 0.2.4 fixes from the owner's completed campaign: faster area loading, Thor's bridge crossing, a more forgiving castle landing, the Moon Sword equipment cursor, first-phase Evil Spirit/Zerah health bars, widescreen enemy simulation and the ending achievement. Local achievements now use compact top-right notices. The hidden cameo retains Lagoon-style typing dialogue, a portrait and uninterrupted mu

Native combat, damage, death/revival, boss services and endgame routes, display fades and guarded HUD dispatch have focused checks; the interpreter remains enabled for paths that have not been qualified. Scenery contracts cover all 75 authored room descriptors, but that does not mean every actor, effect, menu or transition supports widescreen. Unsupported content falls back to the original view.
