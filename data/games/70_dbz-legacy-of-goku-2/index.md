The Legacy of Goku II is an experimental [GBARecomp](/hardware/game-boy-advance) preview.

It is a proof point for the framework, and another place to test adaptive widescreen on a GBA action RPG.

## Playable status

Experimental preview. The USA release boots through its attract sequence and into gameplay. It has not been tested exhaustively through the whole game.

A Windows package is on the GitHub releases page. It runs from dumps you provide: your Dragon Ball Z: The Legacy of Goku II ROM and a legally obtained GBA BIOS.

Adaptive widescreen is the main feature. It expands the HUD and overworld as the window is resized, without stretching the original image.

The faithful 240x160 presentation stays available.
