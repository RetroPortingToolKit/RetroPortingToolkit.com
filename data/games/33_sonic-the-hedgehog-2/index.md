Sonic the Hedgehog 2 is the second title recompiled by [SegaGenesisRecomp](/hardware/sega-genesis), maintained by the core team and announced in the 2026-05-21 write-up. The current release is v0.4.0 (2026-06-17).

## What works today

The game runs as statically recompiled 68000 code through the shared runner. At announcement, stages were navigable while boss fights had known issues.

## Enhancements

A widescreen setting, using the framework's opt-in 16:9 widescreen injection. Framework-level 2-player split-screen exists for the Sonic family.

No game data is distributed; the project builds from your own legally dumped ROM.
