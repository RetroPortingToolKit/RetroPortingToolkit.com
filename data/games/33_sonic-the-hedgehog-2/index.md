Sonic the Hedgehog 2 is the second title recompiled by [SegaGenesisRecomp](/hardware/sega-genesis), maintained by the core team and announced in the 2026-05-21 write-up.

## Can I play it?

Playable alpha. The current release is v0.4.0 (2026-06-17); at announcement, stages were navigable. You build from your own ROM dump.

## What works

The game runs as statically recompiled 68000 code through the shared runner.

## Enhancements

A widescreen setting, using the framework's opt-in 16:9 widescreen injection. Framework-level 2-player split-screen exists for the Sonic family.

## Known issues

Boss fights had known issues at announcement.
