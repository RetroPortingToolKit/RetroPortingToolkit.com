xenogears-recomp is an independent community project by OpokXeno, described as a "Static recompilation of Xenogears for PC". It builds on [PSXRecomp](/hardware/playstation) as its decoder rather than reimplementing the recompilation layer, one of the clearest signs of the toolkit being picked up outside the core team.

## Can I play it?

In active alpha, with releases published on GitHub; the most recent is v0.5.0 (2026-08-17). You build from your own disc dump.
