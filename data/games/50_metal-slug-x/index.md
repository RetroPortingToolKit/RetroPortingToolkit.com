Metal Slug X has a community [PSXRecomp](/hardware/playstation) build by TechnicallyComputers.

It targets the PlayStation conversion of SNK's run-and-gun arcade game.

## Playable status

In alpha. Builds are published for Windows, macOS, and Linux. The project makes no claim about a complete playthrough.

It builds from a disc dump you provide. OpenBIOS boots it unless you supply a retail BIOS.

Use a complete disc dump, not a partial track-only dump.
