Mega Man X5 holds the middle of [PSXRecomp](/hardware/playstation)'s PlayStation X run, between [Mega Man X4](/games/mega-man-x4) and [Mega Man X6](/games/mega-man-x6).

## Can I play it?

Early playable alpha: the game runs, currently at v0.0.2-alpha (2026-08-01). You build from your own disc dump.

## What works

Gameplay, plus FMV decoding with an auto-skip option for those who have seen the cutscenes enough times.

## Enhancements

16:9 widescreen and frame interpolation ship as mods, both off by default.

A community randomizer fork is underway, building on this recompilation as its base.

## Requirements

Your own legally dumped disc image of Mega Man X5.
