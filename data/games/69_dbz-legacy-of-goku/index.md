The Legacy of Goku is an experimental [GBARecomp](/hardware/game-boy-advance) preview.

It exists to prove the framework on another real GBA game. It also shows adaptive widescreen: a wider window can reveal more of the world instead of stretching the picture.

## Playable status

Experimental preview. The USA release boots through its attract sequence and into gameplay. It has not been tested exhaustively through the whole game.

A Windows package is on the GitHub releases page. It runs from dumps you provide: your Dragon Ball Z: The Legacy of Goku ROM and a legally obtained GBA BIOS.

Adaptive widescreen is the main feature. Resize the window and the HUD and overworld expand with it. The original image is not stretched.

![The same stretch of beach at the original 240x160.](./legacy-of-goku-native.webp)

The faithful 240x160 presentation stays available.
