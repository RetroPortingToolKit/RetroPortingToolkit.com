A core project: Gumshoe is one of the ten commercial titles supported by [NESRecomp](/hardware/nes), a light-gun game played here with the framework's mouse-as-Zapper substitution.

## Can I play it?

Playable with mouse-as-Zapper aiming. Builds from your own ROM dump through the shared NESRecomp runner, with save-state slots. Windows is the primary host platform; macOS support is experimental.

## Technical notes

A GxROM cartridge, the one that rounds out the framework's four supported mapper families.
