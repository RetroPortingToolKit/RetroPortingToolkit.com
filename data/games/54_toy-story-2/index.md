ToyStory2Recomp is a community project by PeriBluGaming.

It rebuilds Toy Story 2: Buzz Lightyear to the Rescue as a native program through [PSXRecomp](/hardware/playstation). Treat it as an alpha, not a finished port.

## Playable status

Alpha. Windows and Intel macOS builds are published, both marked pre-release.

The project reaches gameplay. Booting, the disc-detect and licence screen, the title menu and options, new game and load game, and memory-card saving and loading are listed as working. The intro video is not working for now.

The download runs directly. Point it at your own BIOS or the bundled OpenBIOS, then at a disc image you provide.

Experimental 16:9 widescreen is present and widens the field of view rather than stretching the picture.
