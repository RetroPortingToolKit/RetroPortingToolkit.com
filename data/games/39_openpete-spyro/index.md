OpenPete is a community-built native port of Spyro the Dragon (NTSC-U), combining a fan decompilation with code recompiled by [PSXRecomp](/hardware/playstation).

## Can I play it?

Released at v0.1.3 for Windows, with Linux and macOS planned.

## What works

The game runs as a native application. ixbt.games reports play at 240 FPS, and coverage in Time Extension names Matthew Stanley's PSXRecomp as the AI-assisted tool behind the recompiled portion.

## Enhancements

Presentation-only, per the project: widescreen, high framerate, HD textures, extended draw distances, and music and texture swaps. Gameplay is untouched.

## Requirements

Your own legally dumped disc image of Spyro the Dragon (NTSC-U).

## Technical notes

The port is a hybrid: altro50's unfinished Spyro decompilation provides the hand-written code, PSXRecomp covers the functions that have not been decompiled, and SoapyMan's Psycross supplies the platform layer. openpete.com explicitly credits psxrecomp in its "Built on" section.
