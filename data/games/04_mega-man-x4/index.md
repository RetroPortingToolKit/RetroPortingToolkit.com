The X series hit PlayStation with X4, and [PSXRecomp](/hardware/playstation) picks the story up right there. It sits alongside [Mega Man X5](/games/mega-man-x5) and [Mega Man X6](/games/mega-man-x6) in the framework's growing run of Capcom titles.

## Can I play it?

Playable alpha: the game runs as a native app, currently at v0.0.5-alpha (2026-08-05), with a set of quality-of-life options on top of the base game. You build from your own disc dump.

## Enhancements

Experimental 16:9 widescreen, off by default, which renders a genuinely wider 2D field of view rather than stretching the image. Frame blending, a Fast Loading option, and a configurable Damage Multiplier are also available.

## Requirements

Your own legally dumped disc image of Mega Man X4.
