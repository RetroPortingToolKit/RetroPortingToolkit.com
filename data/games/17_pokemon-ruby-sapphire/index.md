Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire carry Gen 3 into the [GBARecomp](/hardware/game-boy-advance) lineup as a core project.

## Can I play it?

Runs as an early experimental preview at v0.0.3 (2026-07-17), the release that added a pre-boot launcher. The cartridge's real-time clock is modeled by the GBARecomp runtime rather than left unimplemented, and the README warns of flash save and RTC quirks in this early release.

## Requirements

Your own legally dumped ROM and GBA BIOS.
