This card links to the article on 1379.tech.

The short version: recompilation gives a game a strong native runtime. Decompilation gives people names, meaning, and a better map of the original game. Put them together and the port becomes much easier to understand and modify.

The article uses Super Mario World, Super Mario Bros., and The Minish Cap as examples of that idea.
