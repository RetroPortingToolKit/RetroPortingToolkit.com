This retrospective connects the early NES, SNES, Genesis, Virtual Boy, and PlayStation projects as one learning process. Each architecture required different translation and runtime choices, but every successful game fed experience back into the next framework.

Matt also describes the practical role of AI in that work: useful for navigating unfamiliar code and sustaining parallel experiments, but dependent on comparison, testing, and a developer who could decide when the generated explanation was wrong.

For Retro Porting Toolkit, the article provides the origin story behind a collection that can otherwise look as though all of its platforms appeared at once.
